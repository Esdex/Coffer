Coffer keeps your files in the Saved Messages of your own Telegram account, encrypted on your phone before anything is sent. Telegram stores them, but only you can open them.

<b>How it works</b>

- Files, their names and folders are encrypted on this phone, then sent to Saved Messages as documents
- Coffer has no server of its own: it talks straight to Telegram with your account
- The key to your files never leaves your phone; a 24-word recovery key opens the vault if you forget the password
- Sign in on another phone and your files are all there

<b>Your files, right inside</b>

- Photos and videos open in Coffer, read a piece at a time and never written out decrypted
- Music plays with cover, tags and the song's waveform, in the background and on the lock screen
- PDFs, Markdown, text and code files open in their own viewers
- Folders, search, sorting, moving and renaming, with changes showing on your other devices
- Keep chosen files or folders on the phone, to open them with no network

<b>Security</b>

- XChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption with libsodium
- Vault password stretched with Argon2id
- File sizes and name lengths hidden from Telegram by padding
- Unlock with fingerprint or face, auto-lock after a few minutes in the background
- Screenshots blocked wherever the recovery key shows
- No analytics, no ads, no tracking
