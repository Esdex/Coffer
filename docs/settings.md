# Settings

Open Settings from the menu on the files screen.

## Security

- **Auto-lock**: lock while Coffer is in the background, after 1, 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes (5 by
  default), or never. Locking wipes the keys from memory.
- **Unlock with fingerprint**: fingerprint or face instead of the password. The phone's PIN does not
  open the vault. Adding a new fingerprint or face turns it off until the next unlock with the
  password.
- **Block screenshots**: no screenshots or screen recording, and the app looks blank in recent apps.
  Wherever the recovery key shows, screenshots are blocked anyway.
- **Hide exact file sizes**: new uploads are padded inside the encryption, so Telegram cannot tell a
  file by its exact size. About 1 to 2 percent more traffic and space. On by default.

## Vault

- **Change password**: asks for the current password first. See [Your recovery key](recovery-key.md).
- **Show recovery key**: the 24 words, after the current password.
- **Delete vault**: see [Deleting the vault and logging out](delete-vault.md).

## Appearance

- **Theme**: System, Light or Dark
- **Pure black**: black backgrounds for AMOLED screens in the dark theme, with frosted glass for bars,
  sheets and dialogs
- **Wallpaper colours**: colours from the wallpaper on Android 12 and later; off, Coffer's own blue
  and teal

## Player

Video gestures (volume, brightness, hold for 2x), controls (lock button, speed menu, repeat), and
music: **Play in the background**, **Show what is playing outside Coffer** and **Waveform**. See
[Viewers](viewers.md).

## Text viewer

Font size, monospace font, wrapping long lines, line numbers, showing spaces and tabs, syntax
colours, and opening Markdown formatted.

## Storage

What the vault holds by kind (pictures, videos, music, documents, other), each kind opening its
files largest first, and what Coffer keeps in Saved Messages: parts of files, changes, index
snapshots, the vault header, and how many messages are your own.

## On this phone

What Coffer takes up on the phone, and the file cache and previews. See
[Offline files and the cache](offline.md).

## Saved Messages

- **Check integrity**: compares the file list with Saved Messages, marks damaged files, brings back
  files the list missed, and removes leftovers of unfinished uploads and deletes, old snapshots and
  old changes
- **Weekly integrity check**: runs by itself after unlocking, once a week
- **Rescan Saved Messages**: rebuilds the file list from scratch. Nothing in Saved Messages changes.

## Account

Your Telegram phone number and username, whether Telegram Premium is active, and **Log out**.
Without Premium, Telegram limits download speed; everything else works the same.

## About

The version, what is new, the source code, open-source licences and the privacy policy.
