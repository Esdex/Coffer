# Deleting the vault and logging out

## Delete the vault

Settings, Vault, **Delete vault**.

Coffer asks for the vault password and for the word DELETE typed out. Then it deletes every file of
the vault and everything Coffer keeps for it in Saved Messages, **for good and on every device**.
The recovery key will not open it again.

- Your own messages in Saved Messages stay, and so does anything Coffer cannot open.
- Progress is shown while it runs. If Telegram asks Coffer to wait, it waits.
- If it is cut short, part of the vault may already be gone. Delete it again to finish.
- Afterwards Coffer stays signed in to Telegram and offers to create a new vault.
- Your other devices say the vault was deleted and offer a new one too.

## Log out

Settings, Account, **Log out** signs Coffer out of Telegram on this phone. Coffer forgets the vault
and its file list here. Your files stay in Saved Messages, and you can unlock them again after
signing in.

On the screen that creates a vault there is a **Log out** too, for signing in with another account
instead.
