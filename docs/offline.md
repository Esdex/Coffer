# Offline files and the cache

Your files live in Saved Messages. Opening one downloads it, unless it is already on the phone.

## The file cache

Files you open, play or save keep their encrypted pieces on the phone, so they open again without a
download and without traffic. Without the vault's keys the pieces cannot be read.

- Up to 2 GB by default, from 500 MB to 10 GB. When it is full, the files read longest ago make room
  first; a file that is open is never removed.
- Settings, On this phone: turn it off, change its size, clear it, or have it cleared each time the
  vault locks. The cache is encrypted, but anyone with full access to the phone could see which
  files were opened lately and how big they are.

## Cache on this phone

To have files ready with no network, choose **Cache on this phone** on a file, a folder (with its
subfolders) or a selection. Coffer says how much it will download and offers a larger cache if it
would not fit. The job shows in Transfers, goes on after the vault locks and picks up where it
stopped. A file that is whole on the phone has a green check on its icon.

## Previews

Small pictures of photos, videos and song covers on the file icons. Each is made once, as the file
comes into view, from as little of it as will do, and kept on the phone encrypted. Turn them off in
Settings, On this phone.

## What Coffer keeps on this phone

Settings, On this phone lists it: the file cache, previews, Telegram's database, Coffer's index and
temporary files. Your vault itself is in Saved Messages.
